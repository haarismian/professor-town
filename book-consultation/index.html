What Happens On The Call

This is not a sales presentation with a slide deck. It's a working session on your specific situation, and you leave with a plan whether or not we work together.

Who This Call Is For

A good fit if you're…

  • ✓ In industry and want to teach — part-time alongside your career or as a full switch
  • ✓ Finishing a PhD or postdoc and targeting teaching-focused roles
  • ✓ Already sessional or adjunct and want full-time, continuing, or better
  • ✓ Ready to do the work in the next 12 months, not "someday"

Not a fit if you're…

  • ✕ Only collecting free resources with no intention of investing
  • ✕ Years away from starting and just browsing
  • ✕ Looking for someone to write your materials for you
  • ✕ Chasing tenure-stream R1 research roles only — that's a different game

If you're not there yet, that's genuinely fine — start with the free masterclass and come back when the timing is right.
